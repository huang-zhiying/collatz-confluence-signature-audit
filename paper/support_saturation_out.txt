Each line: N samples, observed = number of distinct shortest-meeting distances.
The final line per k adds the singletons f1, the doubletons f2, the Chao1 bound
S_obs + f1^2/(2 f2) and the unseen fraction relative to S_obs.

Reading. The support is still growing at every k: N doubles add fewer new values
but never zero. The unseen fraction is 9.2% at k=64 (N=4e5) and 6.8% at k=256
(N=1.5e5); the larger values at k=512/1024 (12%, 18%) track the smaller sample
caps used there, not a larger support. At the common N=1e4 the counts are
879/1654/1979/2801 for k=64/256/512/1024, a factor 3.19 over a sixteenfold
increase in k (exponent 0.42, the slope quoted in the main text) — a fit to
unsaturated lower-bound readings, not an estimate of the true growth exponent.
These are single-m counts; Table S5 averages twenty repetitions with independent
m and a different seed scheme, so the two are not directly comparable
(879 vs 786 at k=64, 1654 vs 1452 at k=256).
